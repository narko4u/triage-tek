The claim: a screening programme can prove, after the fact, what data was used for each decision, which model version produced a flag, whether the person consented, and who approved the contact.

What is real here: the cohort is synthetic and seeded, the risk model is a published points system, and every transition you make is sealed into a hash chain you can verify yourself in this tab.

What this is not: a diagnostic device, a calibrated clinical model, or a claim about health outcomes. It is the accountability layer around the model, which is the part that gets audited.
